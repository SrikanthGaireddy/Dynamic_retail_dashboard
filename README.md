# Walmart Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Walmart Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Excel.

The main objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data and generate meaningful business insights related to sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, and geographical distribution.

The dashboard presents important business KPIs and interactive visualizations that help understand retail business performance.

## Dataset Information

The project contains 51,290 transaction records with 23 columns.

**Dataset Details:**
- Total Records: 51,290
- Unique Orders: 25,035
- Unique Customers: 1,590
- Unique Products: 10,292
- Countries: 147
- Product Categories: 3
- Product Subcategories: 17

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Category-wise Orders
Analyzes transaction distribution across Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.

### 2. Segment-wise Orders
Compares three customer segments:
- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

### 3. Country-wise Orders
Uses a geographical map to understand the distribution of transactions across countries.

### 4. Top 5 Subcategories
Identifies the five product subcategories with the highest transaction counts.

### 5. Bottom 5 Subcategories
Highlights the five subcategories with the lowest transaction counts.

### 6. Sales and Profit Analysis
Uses a scatter chart to explore the relationship between sales and profitability.

### 7. Interactive Filters
The dashboard includes slicers for:
- Customer Segment
- Product Category

These filters allow users to explore different business segments interactively.

## Key Business Insights

1. Office Supplies accounts for the highest transaction volume among the three product categories.
2. Consumer is the largest customer segment, contributing approximately 52% of transaction records.
3. Binders has the highest transaction count among product subcategories.
4. Tables has the lowest transaction count among the displayed subcategories.
5. The United States has the highest transaction count among the countries.
6. Total profit is approximately $1.47 million.
7. Sales and profit have a moderate positive correlation.
